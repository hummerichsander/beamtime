# Section

The page is a column of sections separated by `line` hairlines, each with a short label in the `margin` column behind a thin vertical rule.

- The consumer provides the label and the content. Labels are one or two words, or a time and author, in `code` `muted`: `group`, `readings`, `runs`, `14:20 claude`.
- Every block of a page (header, stats, tables, charts, notes) sits in a section. Don't nest or box them.
- Under 640px the margin folds away and the label sits above the content.

## In the app

`Section` in `web/src/ui/primitives.ts`; a view's labels come from `web/src/bt/labels.ts`.

- The content sits in `.section-body`, a size container for the grid's width queries.
- In a view, each top-level block gets a Section, labelled from its kind. A title that only repeats the label isn't shown again; three or more words leave the margin empty.
- The drawer and history's side-by-side columns have no Sections: too narrow for the margin.
