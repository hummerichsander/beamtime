# Icons

Lucide shapes from the `lucide-preact` package, by the names in `web/src/ui/icons.ts`. Rules in `components.css` on class `icon` draw them hard: `stroke-icon` (1.5px), square caps, mitred joins, `currentColor`; 14–16px. No emoji, no filled icons, and never the only sign of a state.

**Design system** marks the 22 icons in the design system's own list; the others came with parts it has no component for.
